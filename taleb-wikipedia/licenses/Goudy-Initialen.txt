Goudy Initialen (fonts/goudy-initialen.woff2)

Floriated initials designed by Frederic W. Goudy in the early 1900s and cast by
Lanston as "Goudy Initials No. 296". Digitised by Dieter Steffmann
(Typographer Mediengestaltung, 2000) and distributed freely; packaged for TeX
in the CTAN "initials" bundle (fonts/initials, used via \usepackage{GoudyIn}),
which ships in TeX Live's texlive-fonts-extra.

The woff2 here is a lossless format conversion of GoudyIn.pfb from that bundle.
